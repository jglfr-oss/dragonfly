Hard Days

For the moments that hit hard. Nothing here judges you.

Right now

When the craving hits

It has a shape: it rises, it crests, it falls. It always falls — whether or not you drink. You don’t have to beat it. Just outlast it.

Ride the wave

Most cravings break inside 20 minutes. Start the clock and let this one break.

The wave broke. You outlasted it.

While it runs, do one — cravings live in stillness:

“God is faithful, and he will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear, but along with the temptation he will also make the way out.”1 Corinthians 10:13

The way out usually looks like one of those buttons.

Half the time a craving is one of these wearing a mask:

Before you decide

Play the tape forward

The craving only ever shows the first frame.

It is showing you tonight. Make it show you tomorrow morning too — all of it.

Then picture tomorrow morning the other way: waking up clear, nothing to undo, one more day that counts. Decide from there.

Why I’m not going back

Fill this in when you’re steady. It will be waiting here when you’re not — the bad-day brain can’t write reasons, only read them.

Saved. They’ll be here.

Name it

What kind of hard is it?

Tap the one that fits. Small next steps only — nothing here asks for a hero.

Remember

This season has an end

The exile came with a number on it — seventy years, not forever. He disciplines “to the proper degree”: measured, purposeful, and then finished.

And tonight’s craving has a number on it too. About twenty minutes.
